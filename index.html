When something goes wrong

Pause. Save. Tell.

  1. 1

    Pause

    Do not reply, pay, or send more information.

  2. 2

    Save evidence

    Take screenshots and keep usernames, dates and messages.

  3. 3

    Tell a trusted adult

    Speak to a parent, guardian, teacher or school counsellor. Asking for help is the smart move.

  4. 4

    Block and report

    Use the platform's safety tools and change affected passwords.

Remember

You deserve to feel safe online.

Mistakes happen. You can always stop, ask for help and take back control.
